# Packed Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Packed is a packing-list app made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what
information Packed collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short
version: Packed only collects what it needs to work, never sells your data,
never shows ads, and never tracks you across other apps or websites.

## What Packed collects

**If you create an account:**

- **Email address and password.** Used to log you in. Your password is
  handled by our login provider (Supabase) and is never visible to us.
- **Name and username.** Shown in your profile. Your username lets
  friends share packing lists with you.
- **Your packing lists.** Trip names, trip type, dates, length, the gender
  option you picked (if any), and the items on each list, so your lists
  are saved and available when you come back.
- **Map photos and pins.** Photos you choose to add to the map and the
  locations you pin them to.

**Without an account,** Packed doesn't save anything to our servers.

**Location.** If you allow it, Packed uses your current location only to
center the map on where you are. Your current location isn't stored or
sent to our servers. (The places you choose to pin photos to are saved,
as described above.)

**Photos.** Packed only sees the specific photos you pick. It does not
get access to the rest of your photo library.

## Services Packed uses

- **Supabase** stores your account, lists, pins, and photos.
  ([Supabase privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy))
- **Anthropic (Claude)** generates AI-personalized packing lists. When you
  create a list, your trip details (destination, trip type, dates, trip
  length, the gender option you picked, and the weather for your trip)
  are sent to Anthropic. Your name, email, and other account details are
  not sent. ([Anthropic privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy))
- **Open-Meteo** looks up the weather for your destination. Only the
  destination name and coordinates are sent.
  ([Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms))

If you add your own Anthropic API key in the app, it's stored only on
your device (in the iOS Keychain) and sent only to Anthropic.

## What Packed doesn't do

- No ads, and no ad or analytics tracking SDKs.
- No selling or renting your data to anyone.
- No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Sharing lists

If you share a packing list with another Packed user, they can see that
list and edit its items. They can't see your other lists, your email, or
your map photos. You can stop sharing at any time.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Profile -> Delete
Account**. This permanently deletes your account, your packing lists,
your map pins, and your photos from our servers. You can also delete
individual lists, pins, and photos in the app.

## Children

Packed is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
effective date.
